FoodAdvice — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the FoodAdvice game (the “App”) collects, how it is used, and what choices you have. It applies to the App on Android (Google Play, package com.foodadvice.board) and iOS (App Store).

1. Who we are

The App is developed and operated by Entrenier, OOO (“we”, “us”). The App is free to use. Contact: ivanjr.sidorok@foodadvice.ke.

2. Information we collect

a) Player nickname. You may enter a nickname in the App. It is stored on your device. If you play an online multiplayer match, your nickname is sent to the game server so that other players in the match can see it. We do not use it for any other purpose.

b) Crash logs, diagnostics and device identifiers. The App is built with the Unity engine. When the App runs or an error occurs, the Unity engine may automatically send technical information to Unity Technologies, including: crash and error logs, device model and hardware characteristics, operating system version, App version and configuration, date and time, IP address, and device or installation identifiers. This information is used to detect and fix errors and to understand on which devices the App runs.

c) Camera. Some game features use your device camera to recognize printed game images (augmented reality) and show related content on the screen. Camera images are processed on your device in real time only; they are not recorded, stored, or sent to us or any third party. Camera access is requested only when you use these features, and you can deny or revoke it in your device settings.

d) Game progress and settings are stored locally on your device only and are not sent to us.

We do not collect your real name, email address, phone number, contacts, location, photos, payment information or advertising ID. The App has no user accounts, no advertising and no in-app purchases.

3. How we use information

  • to provide the game, including online multiplayer;
  • to keep the App stable: diagnose crashes and errors and improve performance.

We do not sell your information and do not use it for advertising or profiling.

4. Third parties

Technical information described in section 2(b) is processed by Unity Technologies as a service provider on our behalf. See the Unity Privacy Policy. We do not share your information with any other third parties, except where required by law.

5. Data retention and deletion

Your nickname, game progress and settings stay on your device until you change them or uninstall the App; uninstalling the App deletes them. Technical data processed by Unity is kept in accordance with Unity’s retention policy. You can ask us to delete any information related to you by emailing ivanjr.sidorok@foodadvice.ke; we will respond within 30 days.

6. Security

We use commercially reasonable measures to protect information. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure.

7. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version is always published on this page, with the effective date shown at the top.
